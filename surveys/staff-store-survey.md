# Sachar Mobile – Quick Store Check-In (3 min)

Staff and manager survey, built for phones. It's hosted on Jotform: anyone with the link can fill it in, no login needed.

- Fill in: https://form.jotform.com/262992475242061
- Edit: https://eu.jotform.com/build/262992475242061
- Every submission is emailed to: grant@sacharmobile.co.za, deans@sacharmobile.co.za, byronholland@vodadealers.co.za

The answer options are suggestions written ahead of time. Staff tap the ones that fit, then add their own experience in the optional boxes.

| # | Question | Type |
|---|----------|------|
| 1 | Which store do you work in? (Lakeside / Bedford / Nicolway / Sandton / Hyde Park) | Required dropdown |
| 2 | I am a... (Manager / Staff) | Single choice |
| 3 | Your name (optional) | Short text |
| 4 | Why have the last few months been quieter? | Checkboxes |
| 5 | Anything else about why it's quieter? (optional) | Short text |
| 6 | How do you feel at work lately? | 1–5 emoji |
| 7 | What's making you feel that way? (optional) | Short text |
| 8 | How do customers seem when they come in? | 1–5 emoji |
| 9 | What do customers complain about most? | Checkboxes |
| 10 | What do customers expect when they walk in? (up to 3) | Checkboxes |
| 11 | What stops you most from closing a sale? | Single choice |
| 12 | Tell us more (optional) | Short text |
| 13 | One thing that would make your store better | Short text |
| 14 | One thing that would help you make more sales | Short text |
| 15 | Suggested greeting (shown as text) | Text |
| 16 | Would you use this greeting? | Single choice |
| 17 | How would you say it? Your version (optional) | Short text |
| 18 | Your best line or trick for closing a sale (optional) | Short text |
