# Quick Store Check-In (3 min)

Staff and manager survey, built for phones. It's hosted on Jotform: anyone with the link can fill it in, no login needed.

- Fill in: https://form.jotform.com/262992888016068
- Edit: https://eu.jotform.com/build/262992888016068

The answer options are suggestions written ahead of time. Staff tap the ones that fit, then add their own experience in the optional boxes.

| # | Question | Type |
|---|----------|------|
| 1 | I am a... (Manager / Staff) | Single choice |
| 2 | Which store? | Short text |
| 3 | Your name (optional) | Short text |
| 4 | Why have the last few months been quieter? + own words | Checkboxes + text |
| 5 | How do you feel at work lately? | 1–5 emoji |
| 6 | What's making you feel that way? (optional) | Short text |
| 7 | How do customers seem when they come in? | 1–5 emoji |
| 8 | What do customers complain about most? | Checkboxes |
| 9 | What do customers expect when they walk in? (up to 3) | Checkboxes |
| 10 | What stops you most from closing a sale? + more | Single choice + text |
| 11 | One thing that would make the store better | Short text |
| 12 | One thing that would help you make more sales | Short text |
| 13 | Suggested greeting: use as is / change / no + your version | Single choice + text |
| 14 | Your best line or trick for closing a sale (optional) | Short text |
